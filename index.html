How to hit

Swing your mouse through the ball as the ring closes. Hold it right of centre for forehand, left for backhand.
Swing up for topspin, down to chop, across to aim. Faster swing, harder hit. Clicking, tapping or Space also swing.
Your player runs to the ball for you, or move your feet yourself with WASD. Gold ring? Swing up hard to smash.
